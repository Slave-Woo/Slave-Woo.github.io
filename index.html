About me

I am a Ph.D. candidate affiliated with the Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute and the University of Science and Technology. I use Type Ia supernovae, baryon acoustic oscillations, and complementary cosmological observations to assess the robustness of inferences about cosmic expansion.
