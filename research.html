Research

Understanding the expanding Universe.

My work uses observational probes to test cosmological models and the consistency of their predictions.

01 / Dark energy

Cosmic expansion & dark energy

I study constraints on the cosmic expansion history and investigate whether observations require departures from the standard ΛCDM cosmological model.

02 / Statistical methods

Cosmological consistency tests

I use flexible, data-driven statistical methods—including crossing statistics—to compare Type Ia supernova and baryon acoustic oscillation measurements with reference cosmological models.

03 / Supernovae

Type Ia supernova standardization

My current research includes studying the possible evolution of Type Ia supernova absolute magnitude and its degeneracy with inferred dark-energy evolution.

04 / Isotropy

Cosmic isotropy

I am interested in tests of statistical isotropy using the angular distribution of Type Ia supernovae, including dipolar signatures and survey-selection effects.
